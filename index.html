Plain

Calories and protein. Nothing more. An iPhone app, in beta on TestFlight.

Getting help

Email luchaji07@gmail.com. In the beta, you can also send feedback from TestFlight: take a screenshot in Plain and tap Share Beta Feedback.

Logging

Tap Log food. Type a number for calories (“650”, or “650 40p” with protein), search the food list, scan a barcode, or describe or photograph a meal for an AI estimate. Swipe an entry left to delete it; Undo brings it back.

Your target

Plain starts from a standard formula, then learns your real burn from your weight trend and adjusts the target once a week, by at most 150 calories. Settings → How this is calculated shows the math. It’s not medical advice.

Accounts

You don’t need one to log. Sign in with Apple backs up and syncs your log and turns on AI estimates. Signing out removes Plain’s data from that phone.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete account removes everything on Plain’s server and revokes Sign in with Apple. If Apple can’t be reached, finish in iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → Plain → Stop Using. Signed out, Settings → Delete all data wipes the phone. Either can also remove Plain’s entries from Apple Health.
